## Description (max 4000 chars)
```
QuickMaffs is a mental-math speed trainer built for quant interview prep.

Trading firms — Jane Street, Optiver, SIG, IMC, and others — screen candidates with fast timed arithmetic. QuickMaffs recreates that exact drill, then goes further: it benchmarks your score against the bar those firms set, finds the operations you're slowest or most error-prone on, and builds practice that targets them.

FAITHFUL SPEED DRILL
• The classic 120-second arithmetic sprint: addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
• Answers auto-advance the moment you're right — no tapping "submit."
• A clean on-screen keypad built for one-handed speed; no keyboard pop-ups.

KNOW WHERE YOU STAND
• Every run is scored against a benchmark ladder — from a solid start to interview-ready to elite.
• See what percentile your score lands in and how close you are to your goal.

TRAIN YOUR WEAK SPOTS
• QuickMaffs records how long each problem takes and where you slip.
• It surfaces your weakest areas — like "÷ by 7" or "large + large" — and builds an adaptive session that practices them more.
• Single-operation and fully custom drills, too.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• A full history of your sessions, best scores, pace, and activity over time.
• Optional account sync backs up your progress and restores it on any device.

BUILT TO FEEL FAST
• Works fully offline. No ads. No trackers.
• A focused, dark, distraction-free interface.

Whether you're prepping for a trading interview or just want sharper mental math, QuickMaffs turns practice into measurable progress.
```
