QuickMaffs — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

QuickMaffs is a mental-math practice app. It is designed to work fully offline and to keep as little data as possible. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You can use the entire app without an account. Your data stays on your device.
  • If you create an account, your practice history syncs to our database so you can restore it on another device.
  • We do not show ads, do not use third-party analytics or trackers, and do not sell or share your data.

What we store

Account information (only if you create one):

  • Email address and password, used solely to sign you in and sync your data. By default the app uses an anonymous account with no email.

Your practice data:

  • Session results — score, mode, duration, start/end time, and summary accuracy/timing.
  • Per-problem records — the arithmetic problem shown, your answer, whether it was correct, how long it took, and edits made — used to compute your benchmarks and find your weak spots.
  • Your settings and practice goals.

Basic technical context attached to each session, to help the app work and improve:

  • App version, platform, browser/user-agent string, time zone, and the local hour of play.

How your data is stored

  • On your device first, in local storage — this is why the app works offline.
  • In the cloud (our hosted Supabase/Postgres database) only when you are signed in and online. Access is restricted by row-level security so an account can read and write only its own rows.

How we use it

  • To run the app: track your scores, benchmarks, and progress over time.
  • To sync your history across your devices and restore it after reinstalling.
  • That's it. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for third parties, or tracking you across other apps or websites.

Your choices & deleting data

  • Discard a run — on the results screen you can discard a session so it is removed from your stats and history, locally and in the cloud.
  • Delete your account and data — you can delete your account from within the app, which permanently removes your sessions, attempts, and settings from our database. You may also email us to request deletion.
  • Use without syncing — simply don't create an email account; nothing leaves your device beyond the anonymous sync used to back up your own history.

Children

QuickMaffs is rated for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the optional account email you choose to provide.
